# Acceptable Use Policy

**App:** nodepiazza
**Last updated:** 14 May 2026
**Contact:** ilia.agentov@gmail.com

nodepiazza connects people who are physically near each other and lets them
chat. To keep that safe, everyone who uses the app agrees to these rules. You
accept this policy the first time you open the app.

## You must be an adult

nodepiazza is intended for people aged 18 and over. Do not use the app if you
are under 18.

## Prohibited content and conduct

You may not use nodepiazza to create, send, or share:

- **Child sexual abuse or exploitation material (CSAE)** of any kind. This is
  never tolerated and will be reported to the relevant authorities.
- Sexual content involving minors, or any sexualisation of minors.
- Harassment, bullying, threats, stalking, or intimidation.
- Hate speech or content that attacks or demeans people based on race,
  ethnicity, religion, disability, age, nationality, gender, sexual
  orientation, or gender identity.
- Content that promotes or facilitates violence, self-harm, or terrorism.
- Sexually explicit content sent to someone who has not asked for it.
- Spam, scams, fraud, phishing, or impersonation of another person.
- Anything illegal, or content that infringes someone else's rights.

## Reporting and blocking

Every chat has a menu with two tools:

- **Block user** — immediately and permanently stops that device from matching
  or messaging you. Blocking is stored on your device and survives restarts.
- **Report user** — opens your email app with a pre-filled report (the
  reported device's identifier and the recent conversation) addressed to the
  developer, and also blocks the user. Reports are reviewed manually.

If you see prohibited content or behaviour, please report it.

## Enforcement

Because nodepiazza has no servers and no accounts, content lives only on the
devices involved — there is no central feed for us to moderate. Enforcement
works through the tools above: users can block offenders instantly, and
reports are reviewed by the developer, who may take further action including
referral to law enforcement where the law requires it (for example, CSAE).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the
revised policy will be published at this URL.
